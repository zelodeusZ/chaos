# Game Design Reference — Chaos

This document consolidates owner-decided design details available in the shared project history. It is not a complete game design document; unknown systems remain explicitly open.

## Character identity

A character is identified by a Race and a Race Trait. The Race is the broad species identity; Race Traits distinguish variants within a race.

## Seven primary stats and Chaos Emeralds

The game retains seven primary stats. The seven globally unique Chaos Emeralds each represent one stat and its underlying principle.

| Emerald | Stat | Fundamental principle |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Power | Physical force |
| Blue | Speed | Motion |
| Yellow | Durability | Resilience |
| Green | Chaos | Chaos Energy |
| Purple | Control | Precision and manipulation |
| Cyan | Output | Energy release and amplification |
| White | Intelligence | Cognition and perception |

Emeralds grant greater access to Chaos / Chaos Energy in addition to their associated stat. Green has the strongest Chaos association because it represents Chaos itself.

This mapping is the project's design, not a claim about official Sonic canon. Emeralds are globally unique artifacts, not ordinary repeatable loot.

## Chaos-energy pipeline

The established conceptual pipeline is:

**Ambient / excess Chaos Energy → Chaos Shards → refined Chaos Cores → stored or harnessed Chaos Energy**

- **Chaos Shards:** naturally occurring crystallized excess energy; absorbed through the skin.
- **Chaos Cores:** artificial concentrations made by refining and compressing Chaos Shards.
- **Chaos Emeralds:** seven globally unique legendary artifacts; distinct from ordinary Shards and Cores.
- **Chaos Capacitor:** Old World technology implanted near the sternum. Absorbs and stores ambient Chaos Energy and enables artificial Chaos Control, including environmental/body manipulation.
- **Control Gloves:** specialized gloves used with a Chaos Capacitor to direct and manipulate the energy it harnesses. This exact name is user-selected.

### Capacitor limitations
- Capacitors have limited durability.
- Charging and use wear them down.
- They can break in combat.

Do not assume a Capacitor is indestructible, limitless, or equivalent to an Emerald.

## Transformations
- **Pseudo-Super:** artificial transformation requiring sufficient Chaos Energy. It is not a Chaos Capacitor.
- **True Super:** naturally achieved counterpart to Pseudo-Super.

Still unresolved:
- Exact requirements and mechanics for True Super.
- Whether/how multiple Capacitors change Pseudo-Super power or duration.

Do not fill these gaps with guesses.

## Superspeed
- The superspeed input is universally **Q**.
- The input is shared, but how superspeed behaves is class-specific.
- Do not assume every class has identical acceleration, handling, momentum, or movement effects.
